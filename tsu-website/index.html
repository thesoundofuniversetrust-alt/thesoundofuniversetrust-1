About Us

A small sound of kindness that echoes across the universe

Founded in 2026 in Ranipet, Tamil Nadu, The Sound of Universe Trust is a registered non-profit started by ordinary people who believe that every act of help — a meal, a book, a sapling — creates a sound that reaches far beyond one life.

We work for the welfare of society without any profit motive and without discrimination of caste, religion or politics — together with volunteers and donors who share one belief: live together, and build a kinder world.

  • Registered trust (Sub-Registrar, Ranipet)
  • FSSAI registered food service
  • Run by local volunteers
  • No caste, religious or political discrimination

Our Mission

To reach the underprivileged with food, education, healthcare and dignity — directly, transparently and with love.

Our Vision

A united community where no one is left behind — people, animals and nature thriving together.
